# Business fit directory and visual stories

Owner requested and approved this expansion on October 24, 2026 while the Business page was being built.

Build a complete directory for the current offer's useful audience/goal combinations, with distinct shareable pages. Lead with end results in the audience's language. Put the logo in the header and keep Bankeiz in the existing footer. Sell continuity, useful creation, client-facing help, ownership and supported actions. Keep AI credit quantities in finer billing details rather than presenting the purchase as credits.

## Approach

Extend the same existing static public renderer and retained navigation. Use one fit registry and a shared page grammar, with genuinely different problems, examples, outcomes and first-use journeys for each audience. The directory is `/business/for/`; audience routes are `/business/for/<slug>/`. The existing offer remains `/business/`. All paid calls to action reuse `/#account/business`, the existing same-account upgrade session and checkout. No new subscription, proof, valuation or identity authority.

Audience groups: client work; creation; teaching and expertise; running and coordinating; building; personal work and research. Initial fit coverage: agencies, consultants, independent professionals, creators, designers, musicians, authors, coaches, educators, founders, service businesses, teams, communities, events, mission-driven organizations, product builders, developers, researchers, project leaders and busy professionals. This is the explicit coverage registry; new evidence may extend it. Do not claim that every person needs the paid tier or that every fit is validated by paid customer research.

Prioritize acquisition around repeat workflows, an existing budget and Business-specific value: client-facing help, own-domain operation, staff/site coverage and production integration. Broader useful fits remain discoverable without diluting each page's message.

## Visual design

Use the existing logo, public header, footer, Inter typography and white/navy/cobalt system. Generated matte-paper/glass illustrations explain six reusable visual stories: keep context, create from an original, turn knowledge into useful materials, guide people to the next step, build a product, retain ownership and history. Each audience page labels its own visual story in ordinary language. These illustrations are labeled illustrations and are not financial evidence, real account data or completed operation receipts.

Owner clarified that every illustration must feel alive and background-free, with the same palette and instant first paint. Six generated alpha WebP sprites provide separate input, optical lens and result layers. The layers move independently in one twelve-second phase, with an animated connecting thread and optical glint. No panel, frame, floor or image gallery. The existing visibility observer pauses them offscreen; document-hidden and reduced motion also stop motion. The owner explicitly removed all on-page motion controls; visible art moves automatically. Images have explicit dimensions and remain fully visible before motion starts. Navigation, price, buttons and labels remain real HTML. Page-specific styles travel with each public document rather than increasing shared entry CSS. Directory filters load only on selection and work locally, without account requests.

Final coverage is 30 complete fits across six groups. The registry at `src/content/business-fits.mjs` is the exact coverage source, including the initial twenty plus filmmaking, podcasting, accounting, property services, ecommerce, hospitality, startups, collecting, career building and knowledge publishing. Every page carries a distinct scenario, opening question, three outcomes and first session. It also explains the broader subscription, so a useful narrow example never reduces the offer to a single task.

## Acceptance

- Every registry entry has unique title, description, headline, useful scenario, benefits, first-use steps, canonical route and sharing image.
- Every page links to the directory, exact paid review and relevant existing depth; no invented contact destination.
- Monthly price and renewal terms remain clear. Credits appear in expandable billing detail, not headline, hero price bullets or sharing image captions.
- Main Business and directory routes never resolve as usernames; the actual username remains available through `/u/<name>/`.
- New pages participate in retained public navigation, sitemap and existing offline public shell, with no private runtime on cold entry.
- Desktop and mobile evidence confirms logo-only header, readable price/CTA, working directory filters, automatic animation/reduced-motion behavior, stable images and unchanged account entry.

## Evidence boundary

Source code proves the existing primitives and supported operations. Customer posts supply vocabulary, not feature truth or market-size estimates. No invented guarantees of time saved, revenue, universal importing, unlimited AI, automatic payments, universal outside acceptance or competitor exclusivity. Show the distinctive combination through concrete results instead.

Owner refinement: the $149/month offer is framed through the banking and money experience. Each audience retains its tailored work story; every page also connects chosen payment questions, getting paid, source continuity and the value already held. Core proof, ownership and supported value remain free. Business adds intelligence and business tools. The shared banking section uses existing payment/value guides and adds no runtime or authority.
