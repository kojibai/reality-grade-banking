# Proof-native cash: presentation audit

The account’s spendable funds are proof-native cash. Users hold and use money
with its source, ownership and history connected. Reserve and Settlement retain
the exact underlying funding composition; ordinary account actions present one
amount and preserve automatic allocation.

## Scope

Reviewed active application copy, messages, public explainers, learning content,
cooperative/economy stories, share-card source, operating-manual articles and
current repository documentation. Fiat-specific waiting or funding bottlenecks
now say dollars; cash is also used correctly for native proceeds and account
funds. General cash-flow language remains intact.

A valuation describes an asset under its supported method. Spendable funds and
completed sale proceeds have their own financial evidence. Physical custody,
title, licensing, contributed funding and source integrity keep their actual
contracts. This wording does not introduce a fiat redemption promise, new
issuance, external acceptance guarantee or financial authority.

Mechanical cash/rail/redemption field names, protocol units, retained financial
records, frozen law, historical release evidence and external API identifiers
remain unchanged. Note deposit and account credit are described as those actions
in user-facing guidance. The existing once-only consumption remains unchanged.

## Evidence

- Existing learning, cooperative, economy, valuation and docs contracts: 64 pass.
- Source-bound examples and two affected cooperative share cards regenerated.
- Production build: 134 public guides, 37 manual articles, 150 social cards;
  source archive, offline shell, conformance, licensing and public-page checks pass.
- Phone and desktop glossary layout and affected share artwork checked visually.
  The production static guide was used; the generic development fallback opens
  account entry for this unregistered preview route.
- No new runtime calls, schema changes, payment operations, valuation math,
  financial permissions or first-paint work.
