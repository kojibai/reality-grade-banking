# Proof-Native Economy: personal origination review

Source brief: “Everyone Can Originate Value. Nobody Can Originate Yours.” (28 October 2026).

## What the existing suite already establishes

- `/economy/`: goods, work and relationships already exist; one accepted payment can circulate. The farmer/fuel/repair/grocer story and working evidence readers already serve this purpose (`src/content/existing-economy.mjs`, `scripts/economy-circle.mjs`).
- `/together/` and `/cooperatives/`: people coordinate their productive relationships, rather than needing a new proprietary identity for their community (`src/content/cooperatives.mjs`).
- `/learn/earn-from-originals/`: Seal → Accrue → Sign → Unlock. Each Original retains its source and temporal origin; its own PBI signature and public proof unlock supported value (`src/content/learning-pages.mjs`).
- `/learn/how-value-works/`: inspectable valuation, explicit operands, accrual, already-used value and ownership continuity (`scripts/value-explainer-template.mjs`).
- `/standards/`, `/conformance/` and the economy guide: open receiving architecture and independent implementations; Bankeiz is one frontend.

## The distinct missing story

The suite does not yet give personal origination and relational accumulation one clear, visual explanation. A visitor can understand signing and exchange without seeing why everyone originating their own eligible value still leaves reasons to produce, work and trade.

Add **one focused page at `/moments/`**. Keep the established exchange and mathematical pages. A separate collection of near-duplicate explainers would scatter this story.

Route inventory: `/moments/` is absent from the current public-page registry and reserved-route set. When implementing, register and reserve it together, and preserve a matching username through `/u/moments/`, as other public routes do.

## Page flow

1. **Your moments are yours.** Four people, four distinct Originals. Show each source and its own origin. Use the existing asset/proof visual language.
2. **You still need one another.** Farmer, mechanic, musician and builder exchange useful goods or services. Show the accepted payment moving with its source history.
3. **What you receive changes what you hold.** Show different earned, spent and retained amounts. Shared origination law does not imply identical balances.
4. **Your moments are yours. What you do with them is up to you.** Continue to the working economy demonstration or the actual banking walkthrough.

Keep monetary examples explicitly illustrative and preserve one fixed evaluation coordinate while demonstrating transfers. A computed valuation, admitted Settlement and a recorded payment retain their exact meanings. The existing `src/qualification/source.ts` reader binds earning access to accepted account activity; it is not a counter that independently grants funds.

## Connections rather than rewrites

- Link `/moments/` from the economy story where personal origination naturally follows recognition of existing value.
- Add a short continuation on Earn from Originals: “Your moments are yours. See why people still work and exchange.”
- Link back to `/economy/` for circulation, the value guide for the exact mathematics, and conformance for independent receiving.
- Keep Glyph mathematics inside the technical explanation. Public name: **Proof-Native Economy**.
- Preserve the hierarchy: Receiz is reality-grade infrastructure; the Proof-Native Economy is the economic system; Reality-Grade Banking is its open financial architecture; Bankeiz is one implementation.

## Scope of this review

This document records the coverage audit and recommended page. It does not create a new route, change monetary admission, alter origination law or issue funds. The meeting patch remains scoped to the authorized meeting experience.
