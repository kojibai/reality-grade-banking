# Receiz bank visual system

References: `account-concept.png` (853 × 1844), `deposit-market-concept.png` (853 × 1844). Generated with the built-in image tool using the complete account and deposit/market briefs from this chat.

Palette: warm white `#FCFBF8`, navy `#172B45`, blue `#244DCB`, positive green `#18856A`, muted gray `#87909D`. White inset surfaces use fine neutral borders. Chart fills use a very light blue area; other surfaces have no gradient overlays.

Typography: system sans, rounded modern Inter-like metrics. Wordmark 27px bold, bank suffix 17px normal. Balance 44–48px with tight tracking, section titles 20px semibold, body/control labels 14–16px, secondary text 12–13px. Do not inherit browser control typography.

Mobile composition: 20px gutters, compact header, open balance and chart, period controls, two central Send/Receive action buttons, slim growth panel, assets and history rows. Fixed bottom navigation has Account, Market, Deposit, Assets, Ledger and a prominent blue central Deposit action. Chart/data panels extend naturally by scrolling; no nested dashboard cards.

Icons: Lucide rounded outline, 1.8px stroke, 20–24px, colored identity tiles for archive, gold, currency and property. Wordmark is code-native text; charts are code-native SVG. No central photographic assets are required by these references.

Initial viewport copy: receiz bank, Demo, JD, Balance, $24,680.50, +$184.20 this month, 1W/1M/1Y/ALL, Send/Receive, Settlement growth/$184.20, Your assets/View all, Design archive/Proof object/$6,400.00, Gold reserve/Physical asset/$3,150.00, Recent activity, and the five navigation labels. Amounts are labeled demonstration data.

Functional extensions: dark desktop outer surround with the same light console, inspectable balance split, one sheet per action, stock/property handoff choices, market detail and exact-money reviews. No faux phone status bar is implemented because the browser and installed PWA provide device chrome. Deposit is a sheet over the persistent account rather than a separate route, as required by the approved spec.
