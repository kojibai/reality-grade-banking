# Voting and founding charters

An organization starts with its own native Record → Seal Original. Its name is a display field. The exact founding Original identifies the charter, even when several organizations use the same name. All organizations use the same founding flow. Bankeiz and Receiz have no preassigned charter, office or treasury.

## Founding and participation

The founder chooses a starting charter or writes a custom one, names the founders and electorate, sets adoption thresholds and timing, and defines representation, membership, amendments, appeals and spending limits. Offices specify eligibility, number of seats, duties, term, selection and vacancies. Members can add their own articles. The author can edit all suggested clauses, offices and ballot text before sealing. Each named founder signs a separate ratification of the exact charter. Founding confers no automatic office.

Every proposal belongs to one charter. Its voting members can propose after all founders ratify. Policy, poll, election, treasury and amendment forms use the selected charter. Elections bind to an elected office and to the selected candidates’ own signed statements. A poll records preferences. A proposed amendment can describe a successor charter; the successor has its own identity, electorate and ratifications. Freeform clauses and custom appointment procedures remain available for members to interpret and carry out.

A member may grant, revise or revoke a time-limited mandate where the charter permits one. A direct ballot overrides representation. The public count uses eligible direct ballots and eligible pre-opening mandates. Duplicates do not add votes. A conflicting revision chain is disputed. Each count can be traced to the signed Originals in the reader’s possession.

## Custody, publication and public inspection

Bankeiz uses its installed Receiz SDK, native owner-bound record sealer, existing Identity Record and PBI entry, and app-state publication. The standalone voting page and profile panel share the same board. Public standalone records are addressed by their enclosing Original digest. Device-held sources remain available when distribution is unavailable. The directory reads new entries through the existing verification and retention path. Its cursor describes delivery progress.

A signed creation first remains with its issuing identity. The holder can save the complete enclosing Original or publish it. Public facts come from verified public source material. Private identity artifacts, signing capabilities and terminal recovery remain with the holder. A social preview describes a record and links to its source page.

## Binding decisions

The optional unanimous-evidence-closure-v1 rule binds a fixed electorate, a register Original and genesis head, and a named custodian. After both voting and challenges close, every voting member must sign the same list of exact evidence. A missing signer, conflicting manifest or unresolved timely member challenge prevents certification. Each reader can reopen and recount the enclosure. For a register decision, the custodian reviews the exact plan, signs the register append and submits it through the native governance adapter. A committed decision carries the accepted successor head. Historical inspection reopens the committed checkpoint at its signed time.

The registered reducer handles binary policy decisions, seating qualifying candidates in an elected office, and authorizing a single exact treasury installment. Ties do not award a contested seat. An occupied office requires a supported succession transition before another holder can be seated. Text alone cannot change a membership list, override a challenge, move a treasury, or perform a recall.

## Currency governance

A currency issued with editable terms can link a ratified charter during issuance or later from Currency settings. Selecting governance during issuance opens the charter editor in the same form once the signed currency exists. The charter names that exact currency, sets an allowed purchase-price range and specifies whether voters can pause or reopen sales. All named founders ratify the charter. Once their Originals are published, the issuer can review and sign the link in the form. From that point, the charter governs those settings.

Voting members can propose changes and vote from the currency page. Each proposal binds the exact settings it would replace. Buys, sells and transfers can continue during voting. A previously approved settings change supersedes proposals written against its predecessor.

For founder custody, the signed link carries the held starting register so an authorized Receiz worker can reopen it when the first vote is submitted.

For a fixed electorate, every eligible voter signs the same evidence list after the vote and challenge periods. Bankeiz submits the complete approved proof when the signed closure is available. The native currency writer reopens the charter, ratifications, proposal, ballots and closure Originals and applies an eligible change once. No second issuer or custodian signature is required for the currency change. A rejected proposal or a proof outside the charter’s authority leaves the settings as they were.

An open holder charter lets account holders register for each proposal. Eligibility uses the currency holdings recorded before the proposal’s opening Kai. Open the proposal and check vote status to see the registration, review, voting and challenge periods. Bankeiz saves the signed registration or ballot when you submit it, then shows it as accepted once it appears in the register. The charter’s named custodian signs each register update with the protected key bound in the charter. The single founder can select **Serve as the founding custodian** and use their admitted key. Bankeiz retains the starting register Original when the founder reviews the charter, or the founder can open an existing starting register. Selecting an independent custodian requires that custodian’s exact starting register. Once the challenge period closes and the charter’s conditions are met, the native writer applies an approved settings change once. The issuer links a single-founder open charter by authorizing the exact charter and ratification Originals.

## Treasury

A binding treasury charter supplies the budget and installment IDs, source Original and current source head, recipient subject and expected head, and amount in micro-Φ. The proposal carries the same installment. An approved, committed register change authorizes a separately confirmed payment. The existing Settlement writer checks funding, both heads and idempotency. Its V123 receipt records the transfer. Bankeiz displays verified final settlement only after the SDK independently reopens both signed successor transitions matching the intent and receipt. The Crystal route requires lawful native Settlement funding ingress. A wallet balance, a Reserve position or authorization alone cannot supply it.

## Local qualification

Harbor Tool Library is a populated charter with individually downloadable test Originals. The practice workspace keeps additions in the current browser tab. It can exercise founding, ratification, nominations, proposals, polls, mandates, revised ballots and challenges without issuing public records.

Separate local execution fixtures use isolated test roots. A two-voter election seats an elected steward, and a treasury vote authorizes a 25,000 micro-Φ installment. A rollback-only PostgreSQL test applies that exact approved intent to seeded test Settlement positions, checks both resulting balances, and repeats it without a second transfer. A SQL receipt and a pair of publicly inspectable signed successor Originals are different evidence. The fixtures do not grant authority to any live organization or treasury.
