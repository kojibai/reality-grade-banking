## Ledger and public rules

Proofs and actions have the same subtle open/copy receipt controls as transfers.
Canonical card snapshots and consideration are separate. Canonical sigil operands
can reproduce intrinsic value at the transfer coordinate. Live intrinsic estimates
recompute at an explicit SDK presentation-clock pulse and compare with the prior
pulse. Their coordinate is visible. Opaque source values stay carried values;
missing sports transfer snapshots are not inferred from current prices.

The value guide at /learn/how-value-works/ contains scoped motion and graphics,
a calculator using the installed v129 policy, exact constants and term traces,
integer dollar display, held-original verification and export/import of SDK replay
artifacts. It distinguishes intrinsic calculation, market price and spendable
Settlement. Editable illustrations never gain proof authority. Originals remain
on the device. Calculator modules are lazy and included in the offline shell.

Reserve explanations remain concise: funds retain their proof while held;
choosing a legacy payment route moves the receiving balance outside continuity.
The detailed explanation is limited to the relevant guide sections.
