valuation. Historical nonzero Settlement dollar values require their actual
historical valuation sources; the checkpoint rate cannot reprice the past.

`https://crystal.receiz.com`. Live current-source export/publication still needs
that host, the secure Receiz connection and exact reviewed disclosure authority.

response. See [function boundaries](../current-function-boundaries.md).
