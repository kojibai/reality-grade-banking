# Complete an independent technical assessment

This procedure is executable as an institutional audit program, including
controls that require physical trials or authorized private evidence review.
It applies the same published criteria to Bankeiz and any compatible independent
implementation. The project operator does not accept, veto or sell the verdict.
A reviewer must actually inspect the evidence, reproduce the applicable tests
and record the basis of their conclusion.

The [full assessment template](../../institution/conformance-independent-assessment.template.json)
contains every receiving control, its positive, negative and
concurrency/interruption procedure, exact acceptance criteria, retained
evidence, remediation and rerun. Its [application report schema](../../institution/conformance-independent-assessment.schema.json)
requires all 16 control records. It is distinct from canonical SDK schemas and
from the local automated gap-review format. Schema validity, a signature-shaped
field or a reviewer's declared `accepted` is not automatically verified machine
proof of the underlying fact.

1. **Scope.** Identify the implementation/operator, source revision and full
   source/build/resource manifests; actual adapter, trust roots/policy, realm,
   environment, hardware, operations, all applicable rails and both Note
   execution profiles where supported. Identify SDK/MCP/AI Skills and schema
   versions. Separate financial execution, carried-source inspection and local
   synthetic contract testing. A requirement may be marked non-applicable only
   with a normative applicability rationale, not because it failed or evidence
   is unavailable. The reviewer signs off that rationale.
2. **Control objective.** Read the normative requirement and its linked
   implementation/tests. Use the exact original denomination, current ownership,
   source/destination heads and admitted KaiSigil/KKS coordinates. Inspect the
   causal graph; bare wall-clock/pulse values are not proof authority.
3. **Test procedure.** Execute each control's positive, negative and
   concurrency/interruption trials against the actual selected adapter. The
   template supplies the procedure for each control. Repeat across the supported
   rail/profile/device matrix. Use an isolated authorized realm for fault
   injection and preserve the relevant actual trust and durability semantics.
   Canonical custom-root or in-memory fixtures must be labeled; they do not
   demonstrate production funding or physical custody.
4. **Required evidence.** Retain complete originals and closure, independent
   verifier outputs and versions/resources, exact requests/consent, before/after
   source and participant heads, authentic accepted receipts/refusals, complete
   recovery coordinates, relevant device attestation/assertions and actual
   observed state after restart. Index artifacts by exact SHA-256, kind,
   custodian and authorized inspection/access procedure. Bind each observation
   to the exact implementation build, procedure and evidence references.
5. **Observed result.** Record raw values, heads, quantities, event counts,
   refusal codes and state transitions. Compare them with that control's exact
   acceptance criteria. Distinguish absent evidence, unsupported verification,
   unperformed trials, observed refusal and an observed law violation. A local
   PASS on another build, rail or device cannot fill a missing observation.
6. **Finding.** The independent reviewer reopens the complete sources under
   the declared policy, reproduces the applicable cases and records acceptance
   or rejection with their identity, date and rationale. Resolve each artifact
   reference, verify its hash and authenticity and confirm the claimed result
   actually follows from it. A copied report boolean is not accepted evidence.
   Review custody, durable-host behavior and private access in the authorized
   actual environment where a public read-only file verifier cannot observe them.
7. **Remediation.** Document the violated/missing control, concrete required
   implementation correction and the original uncertain operation's disposition.
   Preserve failures and exact intent; do not abandon an unknown operation,
   restore a consumed predecessor or mint a new source to hide the defect.
8. **Rerun.** Reproduce the failed positive/mutation/race/crash trial at the
   corrected exact build, reverify all affected complete originals, and append
   new evidence hashes and observations. Reassess adjacent controls affected
   by the change. Retain the previous finding and material limitations.

Conclude **conforming for the assessed capability** only when the independent
reviewer has accepted every applicable control and every applicability exclusion
is justified. Otherwise conclude **does not pass** or **conformance not
demonstrated**, with missing/invalid/violating evidence identified. Publish safe
scope, source/build/trust identities, test procedures, accepted evidence
references and material failures/limits; keep private originals under authorized
custody. An institution's record must be inspectable by its intended independent
reviewers. Publication is the institution's own authorized decision; these tools
do not transmit or publish its evidence.

The `/conformance/#assess` automated receiving-evidence review assists steps
1, 4 and parts of 5 by checking profile/case/source bindings and supported
canonical originals. It explicitly lists missing/non-automatable proof. The
active contract testbench and recorded Bankeiz integration suites assist step
3. None substitutes for the complete independent program above. This provides
a full assessment path without project permission while preserving the actual
limits of automated verification.
