## What this release makes possible

Bankeiz brings working financial operations, creative work and the evidence
behind them into one connected experience. Receiz supplies the underlying proof
infrastructure. Reality-Grade Banking defines the receiving requirements that
independent implementations can follow.

The changes since version 4 make public work easier to discover and experience,
give merchants a fuller Counter, simplify receiving and make Originals and their
proof easier to keep. Accepted public views, media and calculations remain
available as people move through the application. Existing account authority,
financial approval and exact-operation recovery continue to govern each action.

## What ships in the committed source

### Discover people, Originals and music

The public Feed offers an immersive view of images, music, videos, books,
essays, moments and documents. Discover adds a searchable mixed-media gallery
and People views over the existing public collection. Selecting a work opens
the existing viewer; closing it returns to the retained discovery context.
Feed can also open over Market without losing the underlying collection or
ending a selected purchase or payment presentation prematurely.

User-started music has one native audio owner, an advancing queue, lyrics and
a compact return control across supported in-app navigation. Pause and Stop
remain explicit controls. Books retain their public titles and authorized
previews; PDFs use the existing page-swipe gesture. Essays, carried text,
collectible artwork and proof-backed primary images have fuller public
presentations without making display artwork a substitute for the Original.

Chosen portraits and names remain first. Deterministic Identity Seal artwork
uses the original account coordinate when no chosen portrait is present.
Short handle labels preserve the full destination and identifier. An admitted
owner header can paint immediately without opening another profile, and
private owner presentation stays outside anonymous caches.

### Create an Original, then continue with that same Original

The full-screen creation studio adapts to the selected content and provides
camera capture, primary media, artwork, attachments, text and an optional link.
Its canonical content retains the exact authored description and carried
material. The locally sealed Original is retained and displayed before public
delivery completes, with its original artwork, creation coordinate and value.

Public delivery preserves the exact saved intent, accepted predecessor and
canonical successor. Bounded appends reuse established source context rather
than restoring every old Original merely to add one new child. Interrupted
delivery, lost acknowledgements and already committed appends recover the same
source. Retrying does not create another Original or repeat its backing.

The existing Assets fingerprint control signs the selected Original through
PBI. Its accepted act stays bound to that Original and its authorship history;
signing does not move its origin or restart accrual. Reopening Create can resume
saved delivery, and public display retains compatible carried facts while a
later acknowledgement is resolved. The guide now shows this actual signing
flow in an explicitly illustrative walkthrough.

### A Counter for offerings, stock and the next sale

The quick amount Counter remains available. Its register adds private offerings,
categories, favorites, product images, variants, modifiers, SKU/barcode and
decimal stock. Optional fields cover the product, service, tax and stock details
a merchant chooses to retain. Imports show a preview, match stable identifiers
and report rejected rows without deleting existing offerings.

Saved carts support quantities, discounts, tax, tips, customer/table,
fulfillment and notes. A checkout freezes the exact sale snapshot before sharing
its existing fixed payment link or QR. Later catalog edits cannot change that
sale. Accepted payment originals drive the once-only stock effect; repeated or
additional genuine payments remain inspectable rather than fulfilling twice.

Activity retains sale lines, original payment references, filters, totals and
exports. Drafts and checkouts survive closing the register. Unpaid saved
checkouts can be cancelled, including older requests, while an already accepted
payment always remains recorded. Leaving a pristine register does not wait for
an unrelated remote synchronization.

### Receive by email and send a Note directly

Send accepts an email destination through the existing recorded-payment route.
The invitation brings the sender, amount and reference together. Bankeiz checks
the payment's recorded recipient before continuing the existing identity and
account handoff. Email is a destination, not an ownership credential. Receiving
keeps the same recorded payment; opening or retrying the invitation does not
credit it again or require a new-account ceremony.

New connected Note images carry the complete delivery material needed for a
receiver to form the supported handover, removing a separate receiving-request
file exchange. The sender can bind a Note to an optional person's stable account
UID. Existing qualified Note formats retain their own controls and do not become
bearer Notes through a display or export change.

A received Note can be verified and retained offline. Its received value stays
separate from available account credit until the existing connected claim is
confirmed. Reimports and uncertain outcomes reuse the retained claim. These
changes preserve the supported Reserve, Settlement and combined funding parts
and the separate requirements for protected continuous-offline consumption.

### Inspect the amount, exact units and history

Balance details preserve recorded Reserve denomination, exact carried Phi and
their source-bound calculation inputs. Where supported funding attribution is
available, the read-only disclosure explains the Reserve units without deriving
them by converting a displayed dollar total backwards. The existing Settlement
floor and below-zero source relationship are explained in the account's own
calculation view.

After suspension, bounded exact samples fill the newly elapsed chart interval
so its interior values can be inspected. Older balance detail remains available.
Global Market Cap has nested calculation disclosures, and compatible public
share inventory, quote timing and settled trade observations survive retries,
partial collections and profile navigation. New Originals can extend retained
history without inventing missing supply or historical ownership.

Ledger gains local search and retained search/category controls, clearer share
certificate movements and fuller Original presentation. Existing account history
also supplies supported Twin recaps. A public listed amount or valuation remains
distinct from an admitted financial result and current spending authority.
