# Bankeiz 6.0 — Your work. Your money. Your economy.

**Proof-native banking, invoices, payments and Originals.**

Your work deserves a complete economy around it.

Start with a client conversation. Keep the discussion, prepare a branded
proposal, work through an agreement, create the actual document, arrange a
meeting and send an invoice. Your client can read the Original and choose the
existing payment review. Keep the resulting documents, payment records and
supporting proof connected without treating one as another.

Create an Original for your public profile and retain its complete sealed
Source, artwork, full text and authorship through reopening, signing,
sharing and download. Discover and Feed present the public collection already
held by the reader. Invoices and receipts retain their own exact Sources and
bindings. A displayed price, an intrinsic valuation, backing and a completed
payment each keep their actual meaning.

Bankeiz supplies the account and working experience. Receiz supplies the
implemented identity, proof, ownership, history and settlement primitives.
The open Reality-Grade Banking implementation makes those connections
inspectable and reproducible.

## What ships

### Turn a conversation into client work

The relationship Workspace brings **Twin, Work and Context** into the existing
conversation. Draft privately with the relationship Twin, keep the main message
draft, and explicitly send the reply you choose. Work remains available without
paid Twin access: edit messages, prepare documents manually and return to the
actual files, agreements, appointments and recorded payments.

Create branded proposals and invoices with exact-cent line items and complete
terms. Produce real PDF, DOCX, XLSX and PPTX documents, including editable
sections and tables. Held sender and client details can prefill the document;
missing details stay editable. Preview, editable copy and the enclosing sealed
Original remain distinct outputs.

Agreement requests carry the complete terms and their Original in the
conversation. Each named party reads and explicitly chooses their own consent.
The supported consent Original binds its agreement, terms, conversation and
party coordinates. A message label or detached identity claim cannot establish
a signature or a paid result. This release makes no universal enforceability
claim.

Exact owner and conversation bindings, encrypted drafts and durable uncertain
send records preserve the work through interruption. Checking an unknown send
reuses its original coordinates rather than blindly sending again.

### Create an invoice your client can open and pay

**Receive → Create invoice** opens the existing invoice editor for one selected
client or email conversation. Review the business details, line items and terms,
then retain the canonically sealed PDF. Copy an invoice link or send it in the
client conversation. The issuer can reopen the saved Original after
interruption; the recipient sees the itemized invoice and can download those
same original bytes.

The standalone invoice page opens payment options only after the customer's
explicit **Pay** choice. It passes the invoice's exact fixed-sale recipient,
amount and digest into the existing Card, Balance or Note checkout. Reading an
invoice, sharing a link or sending a message does not charge the client or prove
payment. A shared invoice link reveals its billing details to anyone who has it.

Native Source custody and the existing material transport preserve the invoice
through cold reopening. An unavailable recent-invoice shortcut or optional
post-admission save cannot erase an already-open Original. Uncertain sending
retains the original attempt instead of creating another invoice or send.

### Add Bankeiz payments to an existing store or application

The portable order integration connects an existing server's authorized order,
exact total and receiving account to Bankeiz checkout. Customers approve the
existing Card, Balance or Note payment. The merchant's authorized reader checks
the received payment before its own application delivers the order.

The downloadable `@bankeiz/payments` package includes a portable JavaScript core,
an atomic store port and a Node 24 SQLite example. It is a source-distributed
integration package, not an npm-registry publication or a preapproved plugin
for another commerce platform. The integration reads the authorized receiving
account; it does not sign for customers, send money, create card charges or
claim Notes. Independent operators supply their own registration, consent,
credentials, persistent order storage and fulfillment.

For simple sites, the existing payment button and link remain available from
Receive. Counter retains saved fixed-total checkout. See the
[portable integration and recovery guide](../../integrations/payments/README.md)
and [receiving/payment contracts](../../institution/accept-payments.md).

### Keep the complete Original through creation and delivery

Ordinary creation retains the already-sealed Source and projects its admitted
Original locally. Optional quantity and original-file price are explicit
composer inputs. The chosen public creator remains distinct from the device
Receiz ID; a device label cannot turn the creator's Original into a collected
work.

Reopening reuses the actual held Source, its composer resources and exact text.
Mounted artwork consumers use the shared resource path across profiles,
Discover, Feed, Market and Investments. Bounded presentation windows do not
retire the underlying Source or make an older held file depend on the latest
32 display locators. Changed transport filenames and MIME labels cannot veto
an independently verified identical artifact.

Signing retains the Original's actual PBI append. Saving the original proof
preserves the complete proof-bearing file and carried authorship; it does not
export a weaker inner payload or seal a replacement to repair a display.
Cold recovery reuses exact retained Source and signed publication work before
unrelated remote metadata discovery.

For the ordinary publication path, public Source delivery proceeds independently
of an unfinished private-original transport copy. That copy retains its own
Source-bound retry work after the public receipt, on reopening and alongside a
later creation. A private-copy acknowledgment cannot downgrade an accepted
public receipt. Protected original purchase and download keep their required
file, owner, price, limit and payment operands.

Optional funded creation retains its separate financial review and recovery
guards; its exact release scope remains in the qualification record.

### Market reuses work it already knows

Keep the same chart, history, selection and media while Market does less
repeated work. Unchanged held chart points reuse their existing geometry.
The pricing worker receives the existing numerical checkpoints and required
inspector/sports operands instead of another full presentation copy. Feed
continues to refresh known Source fields without reordering the complete list
when no ID is new; genuine additions retain the existing ordering path.
Artwork also reuses its exact URL/reference syntax while keeping current-owner
preview selection and the existing Source read/mount/release intact. This
removes repeated parsing and allocation, not proof verification.
Ordinary focus reuses the existing pricing worker, and one real foreground
event wave consumes the existing suspension state once instead of repeatedly
restarting the same work. The settled chart and latest queued calculation stay
in place.

In the qualified 4,268-row static fixture, the changed-catalog worker message
is 78.43% smaller, while all prices, capitalization, six history intervals and
inspector names remain equal. The complete Source stays held. These are
bounded local measurements, not a claim about a particular phone's memory or
total Market latency. See the [exact work counts, visual parity and qualification](../verification/2026-11-17-market-held-work.md).

### Keep receipts, statements and Vaults useful outside the current screen

Supported transfer receipt downloads and verification kits carry the exact
enclosing Original and its continuity. The extracted verifier checks those
bytes offline and refuses changed or incomplete sources. Receipt → Ledger keeps
the selected carried transaction visible while a broader projection is absent;
it does not invent missing archive history or add another financial entry.

Account proof statements, public Ledger statements and private proof backups
make their actual source scope inspectable. Vault containers preserve their
own contents and recovery path, with private and deliberately public exports
kept separate. A private backup is not an anonymous public source, and a public
proof copy grants no private identity or payment authority.

Keep existing funded Notes and owned Originals together in a private Vault PNG.
Choose the supported private-image protection or private image plus PBI, and
save a separate recovery Original where offered. Reopen the saved container,
inspect its admitted Notes and export their exact Originals individually.
The Vault preserves recorded funded units; packing or copying it does not
fund another Note, duplicate spendable value or grant onward spending authority.

Pending payments retain their original nonce and support the existing exact
result/cancellation recovery. Funding ancestry, original transfer values,
ownership facts and historical quotes remain distinct from later calculated
display values. Unknown outcomes do not authorize a new payment.

## Compatibility and operations

Upgrade preserves admitted identities, exact sealed Originals, verified
authorship/history, pending financial intents and unfinished transport work.
The account-entry function and its frozen fixture remain protected. Public
view retention does not supply ownership, spendability or permission to buy.

SDK, MCP and AI Skills remain coordinated at 129.5.0. Bankeiz's preferred Native
source adaptations and emitted modules retain their actual manifests and
hashes. Existing v129 originals remain supported under their actual reader
contracts. No package is relabeled as v130 and no immutable published package
is overwritten.

External services distribute the same truth and verified descendants. A held
Original is not disposable if a service, browser index or display locator is
unavailable. Genuinely missing bytes still require their existing bounded
transport; Source authority does not invent possession or financial delivery.
