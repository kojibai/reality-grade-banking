# Bankeiz 7.0.0 — An economy you can inspect

*Reality-grade banking, from the moment you create something to everything it becomes.*

**Create it once. Keep the Original. Follow what happens next.**

Seal an Original in Bankeiz and it’s yours to keep the moment it exists. Sign
that same object. Share it publicly. Find it in Ledger and open the proof behind
the entry. Close the app, come back offline, and reopen the copy you kept.

One Original travels from your device into the public economy. Its sealed time, authorship
evidence, and recorded history stayed inspectable across the journey. The person
who made it can keep it. The person who receives it can examine its proof with
an independent reader.

That continuity runs through Bankeiz 7.0.0. Open a record behind an Analytics
chart. Inspect the charter and evidence behind a governance decision. Download
an Original with the proof it carries. Creation, publication, and independent verification form one continuous
experience.

Bankeiz product version **7.0.0** uses the coordinated Receiz SDK, MCP Server,
and AI Skills **129.5.0**. The [qualification record](7.0.0-qualification.md)
tracks the released source, build, and live evidence.

## Create once and keep the Original

The editor seals and retains the exact Original locally. You can see the
result while public delivery is still working. You can sign the same Original
with PBI, save its proof-bearing file, close the editor, or start another
creation. A delayed public response cannot erase a signature the device already
holds. An older delivery attempt cannot overwrite a newer creation.

An exact public receipt acknowledges delivery of that Source. Discover,
Feed, Explore, Market, Ledger, and the profile reuse the selected public
collection. A transient read failure leaves the held Original available.
A verified addition updates that collection through the existing append and
retention path. Standalone assets keep their artwork and first frame as the
experience attaches. Installed Bankeiz also reuses retained profile appearance
and media on cold reopen, including supported offline reads.

Version 7 protects the existing creation, signing, custody, and delivery
choreography at build time. The release checks match the working implementation
and exercise its behavior through sealing, signing, cold recovery, and
overlapping delivery. This safeguard adds no work to the user's creation path.
A browser that lacks the required Ed25519 operation gets a focused
explanation when proof creation encounters that missing capability.

The experience is visible in one sequence. Open a new Original before the public
receipt arrives. After delivery, find the same Original in the public Ledger.
Open its proof and inspect the recorded moment. Later, reopen your held copy and
inspect the evidence it still carries. Each step has an observable result, from
the first frame in the editor to the bytes in the independent reader.

## See the economy move and open the record behind it

Analytics is the network observatory for the public economy. Its headline
measures follow carried inputs for capitalization, asset counts, Ledger
activity, and witnessed time. You can change the range, move through recorded
activity, inspect share and collection values, and open a record behind a
charted observation. An incomplete traffic reporting interval stays unreported
until an observation exists. A dated record keeps its own date.

Analytics uses the selected collection and retained history so opening a
different range or receiving the next accepted addition can preserve the
values already visible. The collection cap can keep using compatible held share
data while history prepares. The page keeps its tools and content in place
through scrolling and navigation. It has a dedicated branded preview for
sharing the public Analytics page.

Ledger follows the accepted financial heads while preserving previously
carried records. You can inspect executed share trades, transaction sizes and
collectible activity, and see the relationship between a proof and the activity
that cites it. A scheduled appointment retains its scheduled slot alongside
the time the scheduling action was recorded. Market and settlement values
retain their individual source and role.

Ledger shows one Original when Proof Graph and the Showcase catalog carry
the same catalog item. The two carried projections remain available to their
consumers and existing graph links still open their record. Older graph rows
can display time from a carried event pulse. An asset's current valuation pulse
never becomes its recording date.

## Governance with inspectable authority

Open Governance from Receiz ID, a profile, or the public Vote page. The
full-screen experience keeps the chosen public username, the governing
charter, the activity that belongs to the voter, and the records that support
an outcome in view. Signed-in navigation carries the app context back to Bank.

Start with the charter and inspect how a decision was made. The public
directory exposes attributed governance records. Election and treasury
examples ship with enclosing proof objects and independently inspectable
material. A verified decision can carry the evidence required by its
supported execution path. Opening or inspecting the material grants no
execution authority.

## Move through one application

The signed-in experience keeps the Bank shell and bottom navigation mounted
as you move between Bank, public Profiles, Assets, Discover, Explore, Ledger,
Analytics, and Vote. Scroll behavior and dock spacing follow the same
interaction rules. Returning to Bank reveals the retained view. Feed keeps its
dedicated full-screen experience and existing playback controls. Music stays
under other popovers, and Feed keeps its scroll position aligned with the song
that is playing.

The implementation reuses prepared document bytes, the selected catalog, and
existing public readers. It removes repeated decoding and route preparation
where the admitted input is already available. Production build preparation
folds compatible share updates into the catalog append with the saved history
check still in place. This work preserves the
application's exact ownership, visibility, media, and publication contracts.

## Read it independently

The open implementation ships corresponding source and the means to inspect
the supported proof objects. Public pages, offline readers, exact proof
exports, and release qualification make it possible to check individual
claims at their own boundaries. Profile, public sharing, and Analytics entry
can be checked from their compiled output. The release inventory gives every
commit since the exact v6.0.0 source tag, including small presentation, source
distribution, and CI repairs.

This release builds on v6's client Workspaces, invoices, payment links, portable
receiving kit, and private recovery. Pending intents, held identity records,
proof signatures, and recorded settlement retain their original coordinates
across upgrade. The Registry and public Ledger keep their existing explicit
publication and exact-head requirements.

A companion Receiz change carries the selected Original's exact seal timestamp
into the live Proof Graph response. It has its own source and deployment
evidence. Earlier Receiz work on runtime archives, Storage retries, profile
rollover, and Cadence's separately sealed profile artwork also belongs to
that upstream service. Bankeiz v7 does not release a new Receiz SDK version.
