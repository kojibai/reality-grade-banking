## Send, receive, and send again

- **One Wallet across Reserve, Settlement, and mixed payments.** The Wallet shows a unified balance and carries the exact backing of each part of a payment. The local qualification exercised Reserve, Settlement, and mixed legs with fixture funding.
- **Enable offline.** While connected, choose how much of your balance to make available offline on this device. Add to it or move part of it back. One payment can draw across multiple funded top-ups. Existing custody and availability rules govern the enabled amount.
- **Pay nearby by QR.** The recipient can still show their receiving address QR. For a nearby offline payment, they open **Receive nearby** and scan the payment QR shown by the sender. The app offers a pairing exchange when it needs the recipient’s Wallet approval. The scanner is also available when the recipient is online and the sender is offline.
- **Keep the exact payment through interruptions.** A supported distant Send retains its signed payment for automatic publication on reconnect. Nearby payments and their outbound proofs are retained for reconciliation and recovery. The same signed payment can be retried without a second credit. Connected Send stays available while a nearby payment is awaiting completion.
- **Wallet custody and recovery.** The Wallet can use a retained, newer payment proof to recover a local history gap. When it cannot establish the current custody state, the affected offline funds stay protected. Supported Wallet credentials and prior funding apply to this flow. Offline Note enrollment is a separate operation.
