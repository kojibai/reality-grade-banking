# Reality-grade banking 3.0.0

## Value, proof and continuity. Open to independent verification.

**Product:** 3.0.0 · **Receiz SDK, MCP and AI Skills:** 129.0.0

**State:** prepared for publication. The release source is the commit containing
this record and package/lock version 3.0.0. The matching tag and GitHub release
are operator publication steps. [Qualification and publication record](3.0.0-qualification.md).

Reality-grade banking preserves the source, ownership, value and history of
supported money and assets as they move. Its requirements, implementation,
tests and original evidence are available for independent inspection. Any
institution or builder can implement the published contracts, reproduce the
checks and assess its own software without permission or paid certification.

Version 3 brings that public assessment into the product and adds a dedicated
payment page: one experience for a payment link, a website button or a QR code
at a counter. Bankeiz composes these journeys over Receiz's published SDK, MCP
and AI Skills. Existing identities, balances, financial rules and proof sources
retain their authority.

## New in version 3

### A public conformance workspace

The workspace maps **16 receiving requirements, 14 Note continuity laws and
seven capability definitions** to specifications, implementation files,
executable tests and required evidence. Each requirement identifies how to
assess it and what to correct when its evidence does not pass.

Twelve browser verification groups check the supported public integration.
The entrance status uses this same suite and rejects obsolete cached results.
The suite also publishes the implementation's recorded engineering results,
including individual cases and exact source hashes.

A separate **33-case public integration contract** executes a selected adapter
and independently computes expected results. It exercises exact units, Note
and asset components, intent binding, consent, ownership, atomic execution,
retry/conflict behavior and temporal reference cases. A language-neutral
command-line protocol lets another implementation run the same contract and
reopen its full transcript in the browser. It does not accept an adapter's
claimed success as proof.

Production receiving assessment follows the applicable requirements and original
evidence for the named capability, rail, build and operating environment.
Public contract results establish their stated technical scope; protected-device
and deployed financial qualifications identify their own actual evidence.

### An original credential that carries its evidence

A passing public-contract transcript can be saved as a canonical Receiz-sealed
PNG. The original carries the complete observations, implementation build,
evidence digest, holder binding and ownership continuity. Reopening it verifies
the proof, transcript and exact certificate artwork together.

The published Bankeiz conformance record is owned by **`bjklock.receiz.id`**.
Its original is available for download and independent verification. Its assessed
build remains exactly the one recorded inside it; a later product version does
not silently reissue or relabel that proof.

The institution workspace combines a verified public original with control of
its matching private Identity Record. It exposes the exact evidence, supports
rerunning the checks and retains the original for sharing. A public certificate
alone grants no account or sign-in authority. Private Identity Records are not
embedded in public downloads.

### One checkout, wherever payment begins

`/pay/yourname/` opens a dedicated page addressed to that recipient. An optional
`?amount=12.30` prefills an editable amount. A first-time customer on a supported
empty device receives a device identity without a signup form; an existing
identity is reused. A record that cannot be restored offers retry or import,
preserving the original account. Saving the Identity Record is optional and
supports continuity on another device.

Customers choose **Card, Balance or Note**, review the exact recipient and
amount, then approve. Reserve, Settlement and Settlement Reserve Notes use
their existing ownership, funding and receiving rules. The review binds selected
sources, payer, recipient, amount and any maximum card contribution.

Balance and Note payments can use card funding for the reviewed shortfall.
Only confirmed credit continues the retained original payment. A changed
shortfall requires renewed approval; surplus Note credit or a minimum-card-charge
remainder stays in the customer's account. Back and Resume preserve the original
attempt. Checking an uncertain result cannot submit a replacement payment,
repeat a Note deposit or reuse a different checkout's return.

Receive offers both **Send link** and **Checkout link**. Sharing, the website
button and QR use the selected destination. Profile Pay opens hosted checkout.
A customer scanning the merchant's QR uses the same page on their phone; the
merchant checks the incoming payment in their own account or Ledger.

The current payment link is editable. Trusted order-fixed prices, automated
fulfillment, refunds and native card-present terminals require their own
supported integration. A tap-to-open NFC URL opens the same page; it is not a
card terminal. [Accept payments](../../institution/accept-payments.md).

### Clearer originals and a quieter public presentation

Older public audio now reuses the authorized playback source already carried
with the asset. Market, profile and standalone views no longer try to obtain a
separately offered sealed Original merely to play that public audio. The existing
player is reused; paid Original access and proof links retain their rules.

Public headers use the same inline bank geometry rather than a text wordmark.
Conformance headings use “Reality-grade banking,” with neutral requirements,
evidence links and credential presentation. The entrance record describes its
verified scope directly. Capability reports, qualification checklists and device
records identify what was actually observed or required. The shared footer is
retained. Presentation changes do not alter financial admission or pass criteria.

## The complete product carried into version 3

### Identity and public profiles

One Receiz identity connects the account, public `/username/` profile, assets
and conversations. Profiles carry the existing portrait, banner, biography,
socials and named links; owners edit the same profile. The username opens its
identity evidence. Authorship checks follow the supplied evidence.

Collections have one- and two-column views, categories and search covering the
complete available collection, bounded infinite expansion and retained return
scroll. Inline media, asset-price charts and weighted Showcase history open in
place. Owner Insights and private account activity retain their disclosure scope.
New-account Identity Record downloads use native PNG artwork; imported records
retain their exact original bytes.

### Investments, creation and trading

Investments precede held proof objects and Showcase. They include supported
account-wide positions acquired through Receiz as well as Bankeiz: shares,
current holding value, remaining acquisition basis, unrealized return and recorded
trades. Account links to total investment value and starts its asset summary with
recent holdings. Historical charts describe supported holding value, including
purchases and sales; missing basis is not invented.

The adaptive composer separates primary media, cover/poster, ordered attachments
and full text for music, books, images, video and documents. Books keep EPUB and
essay readers separate. Its qualified envelope is 64 MiB combined, up to 200
attachments, with documented per-file and text limits. Optional backing uses
Reserve first and Settlement for the remainder, preserving both components in
one asset's financial record.

Canonical admission adds that same asset to the owner's retained profile and
Market immediately. Pending public delivery recovers the same source and
original backing rather than funding another asset. Buy presets, owned-share
sale percentages, dollar-first sales and exact share entry share the existing
review/execution rules. Confirmed returned positions paint inline without an
extra portfolio read. Unknown outcomes keep their original recovery reference.

### Payments, history and original media

The account keeps one total balance chart with its funding breakdown available
underneath. Insufficient-balance Send retains the recipient and amount, funds
the reviewed delta by card and continues after confirmed credit. Funded asset
purchases use the account; card checkout serves the supported shortfall path.

Private and public Ledger transaction views retain the authored asset title,
recorded amounts, shares, prices, fees, sale proceeds, disposed basis and realized
return where carried. The details link to the asset. Historical source correction
preserves exact matching; valuation and transaction price remain distinct.

Images, actual album/book covers, EPUB, essays, PDF, video and moments have their
appropriate presentations. Public asset pages carry title, artwork and owner
portrait on cold load. Supported social cards use actual covers; supported
music/video streams and oEmbed keep their playback path. Shared links tolerate
inert social tracking parameters. Funded Note PNG originals retain native proof
verification, exact units and once-only receiving rules.

### Conversations, continuity and the open implementation

Existing Receiz messages, groups, calls and entitlement-bound assistance use the
same identity. Selected context and consequential actions retain explicit approval.
Opt-in message/call PWA notifications use the existing sender and open the
appropriate conversation or invitation. Installed-device delivery follows actual
permission and browser support.

Encrypted private retention, original-intent recovery, retained public navigation
and versioned source distribution remain part of the product. The repository
ships the matching public schemas, native source, provenance, independent
receiver tools, GPL-3.0-only license and required third-party notices.
