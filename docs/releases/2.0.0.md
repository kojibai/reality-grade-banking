# Reality-Grade Banking 2.0.0

## Your identity. Your assets. Your money. One connected history.

**Product version:** 2.0.0 · **Receiz SDK, MCP and AI Skills:** 129.0.0

**Release source:** version 2.0.0.
[Verification and publication record](2.0.0-qualification.md).

---

Reality-Grade Banking connects what you own with the evidence that makes it
inspectable: its origin, its ownership, its value and the history of what changed.

Version 2 brings that architecture into a more complete everyday experience.
Your public identity becomes a destination. Your collection can be experienced
where it is shared. Your investments have their own view. Creating an asset
brings its original, artwork, attachments and optional backing together. A
payment can continue from the money already in your account to the card funding
it needs, without making you start over.

Bankeiz is the live branded reference implementation. Reality-Grade Banking is
the open banking architecture, receiving standard and inspectable implementation
published here. Receiz supplies the underlying proof infrastructure. This release
extends that shared foundation without creating another identity authority,
account balance, financial ledger or messaging network.

## An identity people can visit

Every public profile has a home at `/username/`, with the existing Receiz
portrait, banner, name, biography, website and social links. Additional named
links open in a compact list. Owners can edit the same profile from Bankeiz;
changes remain in Receiz rather than a second directory.

The ID popover offers direct access to the profile and compact portrait and
username editing, including availability feedback. The username opens its
identity evidence; the blue check follows the authorship evidence actually
supplied by Receiz. Asset ownership details and conversation names and portraits
lead to the same Bankeiz profile.

Collections support one- and two-column layouts, full-collection categories and
search, prices, movement and inline media. Scrolling reveals bounded windows
before their boundary while retaining the cards already displayed. Categories
include represented content beyond the first window. Returning from an asset
keeps the collection, images, selected view and scroll position.

An asset's price opens its chart in place. The collection movement opens a
weighted growth history with period controls. Public Activity has its own
readable presentation; owner Insights reuses available Receiz engagement
statistics. Private balances, private transactions and messages stay outside
the public profile. [Profile contract](../PUBLIC_PROFILES.md).

## Investments you can follow

Assets opens with account-wide Investments, including supported shares acquired
through Receiz as well as Bankeiz. Exact owned quantities, current holding value,
remaining acquisition basis, unrealized return and recorded trades are available
together. Individual holdings retain Buy ownership and Take cash.

Account now links directly to total investment value. Its asset summary starts
with the three most recent purchases still held, then public Showcase; Showcase
remains the default when there are no holdings. The main balance chart uses one
total, with funding detail in the existing balance view.

Confirmed trade results update the shared holding immediately after validation
and durable retention. A complete sale removes the held quantity without erasing
its trade record. No second portfolio request is needed to paint the returned
position. Equivalent background refreshes retain the completed investment curve,
preventing a brief flat fallback.

Historical investment charts describe supported holding value, including changes
from purchases and sales. They are not labeled as investment performance.
Missing basis or unsupported history stays explicit; a public Showcase valuation
does not become a purchased holding or spendable cash.
[Investment and execution details](../market-trade-execution.md).

## Create the complete asset

The Bankeiz composer distinguishes the primary original, cover or poster,
ordered attachments and full text. Music, books, images, video, documents and
text receive the fields suited to their content. A book can carry its EPUB and
a separate long-form essay without forcing either into the other reader.

The qualified authoring envelope is 64 MiB combined, up to 200 attachments,
64 MiB for an audio/video/PDF primary, 16 MiB for other primary files and each
attachment, 12 MiB for artwork and 100,000 text characters. These are effective
implementation limits, not a claim of unlimited files or universal codecs.

Optional backing takes one amount. Automatic funding uses available Reserve
first and Settlement for the remainder, preserving both exact components in the
original financial record. An unbacked Original remains available. Backed content
binds the authored source to the same native financial asset and its public
certificate; it does not create an unrelated second listing or expose private
execution receipts.

Canonical admission places the new asset into the owner's retained profile and
Market view immediately. Public delivery has an explicit pending state until
the anonymous source and discovery projection succeed. An interrupted delivery
reopens the same retained source and placement. A backed retry recovers the
original funding before continuing publication; it cannot fund another asset.

The composer is Bankeiz UI over supported Receiz source, publication and asset
contracts. Its integration retains the same identity, exact source and financial
result throughout. [Publication contract](../public-asset-publication-v129.md)
and [composer qualification](../asset-composer-qualification.md).

## A shorter path from intention to payment

An adequately funded purchase uses the existing account. A known unfunded public
buyer enters an amount and proceeds to the existing secure card checkout in
place. Confirmed, account-bound credit continues the approved purchase once;
the customer does not have to fund and then manually buy the asset again.

An approved Send with insufficient funds opens card checkout for the missing
amount. A $10 Send with $3 available needs $7 by card. Confirmed credit continues
the original $10 payment to the original recipient. Back to send restores the
retained form. Existing minimum-card rules show any amount that will remain in
the account. Unknown transfer outcomes recover their original reference rather
than opening a replacement payment. [Card-funded Send](../card-funded-send.md).

Receive now provides a payment link, copyable website button, existing Receiz
checkout link and embed code. The Get paid guide covers installation without
code. A typed developer example prepares the existing card checkout, retains
its session, checks the exact merchant and amount, and confirms receiving credit
before once-only fulfillment. Current Reserve, Settlement and combined Notes
can fund a Bankeiz account payment through the same Pay with a Note option.

These are deliberately distinguished integration paths. The Receiz widget's
legacy Wallet Note token option is not the native Bankeiz Note reader. A
dedicated Bankeiz guest checkout and merchant point-of-sale session are the next
design phase, not capabilities silently added to this release.
[Payment integration reference](../../institution/accept-payments.md).

## Notes that carry their verification with them

Note PNG exports now use the existing canonical Receiz document seal around
their complete native financial source. A saved Note can meet the unchanged
offline image-verification contract while retaining its creation and deposit
behavior. Image verification and native deposit remain separate checks: a valid
image does not itself grant spend authority or repeat account credit.

Interrupted image production resumes the same funded Note. It does not issue
another Note, replace its funding record or weaken Receiz's verifier. Reserve,
Settlement and combined funding retain their existing exact source composition.

## Buying and selling that preserve their place

Market, Investments, public Explore and standalone assets use the existing
ownership actions. The asset and chart remain underneath a trade sheet; closing
the sheet returns to the same object. Returning from a standalone page restores
the signed-in Market's position as well as public Explore's retained position.

Buy offers amount shortcuts. Take cash defaults to a dollar amount, offers exact
share quantities and shows 10%, 25%, 50%, 75% and All for an owned holding. These
controls fill the existing form without requesting another quote or executing
a trade. The redundant Exchange entry is removed.

Trade review uses one live quote. A complete, validated successful trade response
supplies its original result without an unconditional follow-up GET. Financial confirmation
still depends on the actual writer and service response; the interface never
paints an unconfirmed financial result as success.

Trade amounts preserve the exact units and rounding required by the Receiz
execution contract. Available funds, liquidity and representable final units
govern execution; the displayed amount cannot bypass those checks.

An unconfirmed trade retains its owner, nonce and exact request. Check trade
outcome is read-only. Cancel unconfirmed trade closes the original attempt through
the supported Receiz operation: an already-committed original is returned, or a durable
closure prevents that nonce from later executing. A service outage keeps the
attempt pending; local dismissal cannot establish cancellation.

## A Ledger that explains the transaction

Asset Purchase and Asset Sale appear as Transactions in private and public
Ledger views. They show the recorded amount, exact shares, authored asset title,
execution price, fees and the applicable total or proceeds. Expanded details link
to the standalone asset. Sales show recorded disposed basis and realized dollar
and percentage return when that source exists.

Historical public actions recover those display facts only from an exact
matching original trade. Remaining position quantity cannot stand in for shares
sold, and the whole asset's chart cannot stand in for a share-sale result.
Expanded value cards show the valuation pulse once beside Value at, without
changing their calculation or history. Thin updates cannot erase complete
retained trade facts. Original hashes and
financial effects remain unchanged. Unknown basis is never treated as zero.
[Ledger evidence](../verification/2026-10-21-ledger-asset-trades.md).

## Originals that open where they are shared

Authored titles now lead Market, Explore, profile collections, standalone pages,
Ledger details and sharing images. Caption fallback uses its opening sentence
when no authored title exists; the complete caption remains available.

Image assets start with their actual public image. Older sources reuse the
canonical v129 reader and their carried display media without asking for a
separately sold Original. Protected Original access remains distinct from public
preview and proof verification. Music, video, PDFs, EPUBs and long-form content
reuse their appropriate reader; EPUB reading and long-form essays remain
separate. Moment cards receive a complete light presentation and compact feed
layout. Artwork is retained across popover closure and reused from the feed.

Shared music and video URLs expose the existing supported streams, a lightweight
player and oEmbed discovery. Normal opening still leads to the standalone asset;
the receiving platform decides whether to embed playback. Share images use each
asset's actual supported cover inside the existing floating glass treatment,
with a bounded fallback when artwork cannot be prepared. Image preparation runs
only on the share-image route, outside app loading and playback.

Cold standalone HTML carries the title, artwork and owner portrait. The portrait
uses the same URL and request mode across first paint and React, without waiting
for a profile response or adding a profile lookup. Shared URLs tolerate inert
Instagram and campaign tracking while continuing to reject conflicting source
selectors. Canonical URLs remain clean.

## The same identity in every conversation

Incoming replies reuse the existing conversation portrait. Eligible assistant
replies show typing activity before generation completes, through the existing
message stream. Conversation and group membership provide direct profile
navigation without another profile lookup per bubble.

Messages offers opt-in phone notifications through Receiz's existing Web Push
sender. A message opens its conversation; an active call notification opens its
invitation. Account binding, revocation and notification preferences remain
explicit. Delivery follows the browser and operating system; this does not
introduce native Phone/CallKit ringing. Existing in-app call continuity remains
available. [Notification contract](../pwa-notifications.md).

New accounts save the native Receiz Identity Record PNG with their portable
identity carried intact. Existing imported records retain their exact bytes.
Frontend language uses Identity Record, while retained formats and signing
authority remain compatible.

## The open architecture ships with the experience

Version 2 includes the application, public receiving requirements, institution
examples, original schema catalog, coordinated SDK/MCP/AI Skills references,
Conformance workspace, retained native sources, source inventory and complete
corresponding-source download. Build checks cover licensing, resource provenance,
public metadata, routing and the integrity-checked offline shell.

Product version 2.0.0 does not relabel a Receiz proof or upgrade the coordinated
packages beyond 129.0.0. Existing identity records, Note formats, receipts,
encrypted custody and pending operation references remain part of the upgrade
and rollback contract. The application and original documentation remain
GPL-3.0-only; separate third-party rights and trademark/conformance policies
remain in force.
