# Bankeiz 9.0.0 · Make your own economy

**Product 9.0.0 · Receiz SDK, MCP Server, and AI Skills 129.5.0**
[Qualification and publication record](https://github.com/kojibai/reality-grade-banking/blob/v9.0.0/docs/releases/9.0.0-qualification.md)

**Turn your work into ownership people can hold and trade. Issue a currency your
community can use.**

Sell shares in your work or transfer an exact piece of the Original. Give
customers service credits, coupons, or rewards, and set the terms that govern
them. Take a payment in person when the internet is down. The same account
shows what you own, where funds came from, and when you can use them.

Bankeiz 6 put your work, money, and economy together. Version 7 made that
economy inspectable. Version 8 kept value moving. Version 9 gives you the tools
to build and run an economy around your own work or community.

## Ownership you can send

- **Send market shares.** Open **Send ownership** from a verified holding and
  choose how many shares to transfer. The recipient can hold or transfer those
  shares and can sell them where the market supports a sale.
- **Send an Original or part of it.** Whole and piece transfers commit against the
  same ownership history when connected. Send all the bytes you hold by default,
  or choose exact byte ranges under **Advanced**. Share the transfer privately
  with the recipient so they can open it from **Add → An asset**. Holders can
  see the amount and ranges they own. The complete, matching set of pieces can
  reconstruct the exact Original.
- **Work with the proof you already hold.** Copy your Original receiving address
  in Bankeiz. Buy eligible Originals through Bankeiz checkout. The selected
  Original, its evidence, and its history stay together across these actions.

## Issue and use a currency

- **Start with a use.** Open **Add → An asset → Issue a currency** and choose
  **Currency**, **Service credit**, **Coupon**, or **Rewards**. Set the name,
  symbol, supply, how many units to keep, and how many to offer publicly. If you
  keep units, start with a funded liquidity pool. Trades change the pool ratio
  and its quoted price. A launch without kept units can use a fixed offer under
  the supported issuance terms.
- **Use what you hold.** In **Assets → Currencies**, see unit holdings and the
  current dollar value when a quote is available. Send units or trade them using
  the available market liquidity. Currency pages show the price, chart, trades,
  supply, and applicable liquidity and issuance terms.
- **Manage a currency where it trades.** Creators use the currency detail page
  to manage the actions their issuance terms permit. Where governance is enabled,
  open the linked charter and voting from that same page. Accepted decisions
  apply through the signed charter’s supported rules.
- **Make a redeemable currency Note.** From an existing currency, set aside units
  you own and create a Note with its own artwork. **Receive → Deposit a note**
  and **Add → Notes** both accept settlement and currency Notes. Bankeiz reads
  the original and routes it to the matching deposit flow.

## Pay nearby and keep a usable record

- **One nearby payment QR.** An ordinary Wallet-balance payment can be handed
  over by QR when the sender is offline. The recipient can save it on the spot
  and connect to confirm and use that portion. The sender can remain offline
  during confirmation. When a payment combines ordinary balance with supported
  **Offline Cash**, the QR carries both portions. The recipient sees what they
  can use right away and what becomes available after confirmation. Receive has
  one scanner, with a camera and a QR-image option.
- **Send at a distance.** A supported payment queued while offline keeps the
  approved intent and delivers when the sender reconnects. The same payment
  remains available for recovery and retry.
- **Pass a funded Note forward.** The holder can pass the Note onward offline
  while the funding remains tied to the original proof. The final holder
  can deposit an ordinary funded Note when they connect. When creating a Note while
  connected, leave it open or name a recipient. An Open Note is deposited by
  the first eligible holder to present it successfully. Protected custody has
  its own rules for offline creation and use.
- **Recover the exact payment.** Bankeiz retains signed Wallet debit records and
  can verify saved recovery files offline. A matching current witness and signer
  are required before the recovered Wallet can sign another offline debit.
  Recovery after an erased installation may require reconnecting to restore
  signing access. The qualification record gives the exact iPhone test result.

## Follow the funds and get back to work

- **See the source of a funding credit.** Open its details to trace the incoming
  funding and the Reserve or Settlement contributions using history carried
  with the credit. Ledger shows currency activity when present. Market and
  Analytics preserve their held results during refreshes and use the available
  deterministic prices and collection caps.
- **Add money, read, and message with less friction.** Add money has a focused
  funding panel. **Earn from Originals** remains on the first Add panel. Messages
  fills the mobile screen. Voting shows local time and Kai pulse. Learn,
  Conformance, Meet, and the Kai Klok header use the shared appearance and
  navigation updates.
