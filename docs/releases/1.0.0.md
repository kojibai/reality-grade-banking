# Reality-Grade Banking 1.0.0

## Reality-Grade Banking. Money, assets and proof in one open system.

**Release source:** version 1.0.0.
[Verification and deployed qualification](1.0.0-qualification.md).

**Receiz:** SDK, MCP and AI Skills 129.0.0. Product release versioning is
independent of protocol/package versioning. The reference application and its
lockfile identify version **1.0.0**.

---

Bankeiz brings Reality-Grade Banking into everyday use in the proof-native economy.

Open your account. Bring money in. Send a payment. Receive a Note. Own an asset.
Look behind any supported result and follow the evidence that explains it:
where it began, who acted, what changed and what remains owned.

The same system supports the person using an account, the creator publishing
an original, the business paying a supplier and the institution verifying a
received source. Each gets an experience suited to its task. The underlying
identity, exact value, ownership and history remain connected.

This first major repository release packages that work as an open foundation:
the complete live Bankeiz reference application, public receiving requirements,
independent verification, matching source, integration materials and
reproducible release evidence. It is built directly on Receiz. It demonstrates
Receiz in use while providing a distinct product people and organizations can
use, inspect and build upon. Receiz provides the underlying proof infrastructure.
Reality-Grade Banking identifies the open banking architecture and shared
requirements; Bankeiz identifies the branded implementation.
Other operators can build against the same open foundation.

## What changes when the proof stays with the money

An amount becomes more useful when its supporting record travels with it.

A recipient can inspect the actual payment result. An asset owner can open
the original and its ownership history. A business can keep a supplier payment
connected to its receipt and source basis. A receiver can verify the supported
evidence independently. An engineer can inspect the implementation and
reproduce its checks.

Those possibilities share one foundation: a presentation does not replace the
original source. A screenshot, listing or synchronized total cannot create
ownership, backing or another financial effect.

Bankeiz makes that foundation usable through ordinary actions—Send, Receive,
Deposit, Buy ownership and Take cash—with the detail available when it matters.

## What ships

### One account for money and assets

The account brings proof-backed Reserve and proof-native Settlement into one
available balance. People enter an amount; automatic funding uses available
Reserve first and Settlement only for the remainder. The exact composition
remains in the original transaction.

Receiz ID provides continuity through device-held identity. Existing account
access, messages and their supported permissions belong to that same identity.
Payments and other consequential actions retain their exact review and approval.

The balance and its source detail are presented at different depths. The main
view stays familiar. The underlying units, backing, source moment and history
remain available for inspection.

### Send, receive and keep the result

Payments use the existing Receiz Wallet path: recipient discovery, exact quote,
explicit approval, retained operation coordinate and recovery of the original
outcome. Contact suggestions and profile search reuse the existing network.

Receiving links identify the account and can prefill the amount for review.
They do not silently send funds. Embedded card deposits stay within the product
experience while confirmed account credit remains a distinct financial result.

My history clearly separates incoming and outgoing value. All, Sent and
Received views use supported account direction. Familiar names identify Money
Sent, Money Received, Asset Purchase, Asset Sale and the other admitted events.
The public Ledger and receipt details use the same plain names while keeping
public activity neutral about the viewing account. Reserve, Settlement and
combined Notes share clear creation, deposit and refund titles.

A delegated action stays within its associated payment's detail, so the
authorization and financial movement can be inspected together without looking
like two separate payments.

### Notes that preserve their source

One creation flow produces a Reserve Note, Settlement Note or Settlement
Reserve Note according to the actual funding. No rail picker is required.

- **Reserve Note:** retains its admitted funded denomination and backing.
- **Settlement Note:** retains exact Phi; its dollar presentation follows the
  supported deterministic rate.
- **Settlement Reserve Note:** carries both signed funding components and their
  exact split—Proof-backed Native value.

Connected creation uses existing account authorization and the native
once-only writer. It does not require offline-custody enrollment. A completed
recipient-bound original can be verified and retained offline; its account
credit reconciles on connection before funding another account payment or Note.

Continuous fully disconnected creation and onward transfer retain the
separate protected-custody, prepared-resource and irreversible-consumption
requirements. The optional device evidence check exposes the actual response.
Neither a synced passkey nor a local counter label substitutes for admissible
rollback resistance.

Confirmed Note creation and deposit are projected into My history from their
retained verified receipts. The corresponding Receiz host correction projects
modern Reserve, Settlement and composite movements into private and global
Wallet Ledger readers. Original movement IDs, funding amounts, carried rates,
coordinates and source digests remain intact.

An exact repeated deposit is reported as already deposited. Interrupted or
unknown outcomes keep their original signed attempt for recovery.

### Assets as complete objects

Public originals have dedicated browser pages with their own share/copy
actions, asset-specific social previews and supported ownership controls.

Media, supported live value, original source, ownership and history are
presented together. The object can turn to its proof back, where the carried
live-player manifest material remains available: source coordinate, circuit
and proof identifiers, claim/media hashes, attachments, signatures and the
applicable verification resources.

The original media element stays mounted while the object flips. Audio has
refined native controls, artwork, progress, seeking and playback information.
Proof inspection and complete-source download remain available at the
appropriate depth.

Standalone Buy ownership and Take cash reuse the existing Market action.
A new or empty account can enter the existing account/funding path and return
to the same asset and retained purchase context. Funding credit and the
subsequent reviewed trade remain separate; the application does not infer a
purchase from a checkout callback.

Public sealing retains one exact original and publishes it through the owner's
Receiz Showcase and existing discovery projection. Interrupted delivery resumes
that original rather than resealing a substitute.

### An economy people can inspect

Explore organizes assets by useful content categories, including Music, Images,
Videos, Books, Essays, Moments, Collectibles and Documents.

Global Market Cap presents its supported asset universe, source timing and
valuation method. Historical opening values, dated evidence and deterministic
projection retain their distinctions. A computed valuation is not represented
as an executed price, available cash or guaranteed proceeds.

The public Ledger connects events to their available value basis, participants,
source material and receipt. Its local Memory Crystal saves the already-held
Ledger result and display context; saving does not refetch the same collection
or require another financial service.

Source-of-funds detail can connect a payment to several funding events or
proof objects. For a pooled balance, calculated FIFO attribution consumes the
oldest supported available source and carries its remainder into later spends.
The view identifies the calculation rather than pretending the original
transfer recorded a different allocation.

### Conversations within the same account

Existing Receiz conversations, contacts, groups, typing and call capabilities
are composed within the Bankeiz interface.

Assistant availability follows the real account entitlement. People see a
consistent conversation surface; an unavailable entitlement produces a
retained, tasteful explanation and the supported upgrade path when they ask.
Chosen context and consequential approval remain explicit.

An active call can minimize while someone closes Messages, switches Bank views,
visits retained public pages or opens another sheet. The same controller,
streams and media elements remain in use. A compact, draggable window provides
return, microphone, camera and explicit leave controls. Returning restores the
actual Bank surface, its styles and the full call panel.

This is an in-application lifetime guarantee. Browser/OS suspension, network
loss, closing the browser or terminating the PWA are not converted into a
promise of an independent background-call runtime.

### Clear explanations at every depth

The public guides explain the product, economy, assets, receipts, valuation,
security and receiving requirements. Audience-specific pages show concrete uses
for people, businesses, independent professionals, creators, communities,
institutions, public-sector teams and platforms.

Each uses the existing responsive page system, relevant original motion and
interactive explanations. Known public pages navigate through the retained
document and existing runtime; guides do not introduce account work, private
data or a separate application framework.

The grouped footer, guide directory, individual sharing metadata and canonical
page structure make the material discoverable without adding another layer to
the everyday account experience.

## Continuity is part of the operating model

Known verified sources are retained and reused. New supported information
arrives as an addition. A background refresh does not erase a stronger known
result or replace a settled screen with an empty one.

Private local inbox and history retention use the existing encrypted account
custody. Source bytes, receipts and pending financial intents remain bound to
their original identity and operation. The public offline shell contains
hash-checked public application resources; financial APIs and private account
storage are excluded from that public cache.

Updates preserve retained custody and pending operations. An uncertain reply
does not justify a second debit, a second credit or a fresh financial intent.
Recovery resolves the existing operation.

## Engineering corrections in this release

- Modern Note Ledger projections preserve all funding rails and exact split
  operands instead of applying legacy Settlement defaults.
- Public and private pagination preserve exact source coordinates, timestamp
  microseconds and category/tie positions. Same-moment records remain reachable.
- Conditional reads evaluate the final merged page, so a prior SQL-only ETag
  cannot hide a newer admitted Note.
- Cached immutable source ordering and bounded page selection precede
  expensive proof formatting. A one-row page does not format a 10,000-Note
  prefix on every request.
- Native source refusal stays a refusal; it cannot become a silent weaker
  financial-source fallback.
- Public asset routes serve the standalone HTML inline, including rewritten
  route forms, while image and HEAD behavior retain their own response paths.
- Call presentation no longer owns the active transport lifetime. Public-page
  return uses the existing SPA navigator rather than changing a public hash.
- Original asset media survives proof flips and full-view changes without
  another original fetch or a duplicate player.

## Verification and publication

The [qualification record](1.0.0-qualification.md) is the release evidence
ledger. It records local tests, independent review, browser behavior, source
distribution and the deployed/device checks required for the published scope.

The Note Ledger correction passed 55 independently rerun focused tests,
separate native writer fixtures, scoped lint and zero introduced scoped
TypeScript diagnostics. Repository-required governance controls passed after
application. No real funds were moved by those checks.

Call lifetime and restoration were exercised at 320, 390, 430 and 1280 pixels
using synthetic media and the actual application navigation path. The same
root, controller and media survived close, navigation, other modals and
restoration. Those checks establish lifecycle and presentation, not a new claim
about every network, browser or physical device.

Final commands, exact build/source hashes and authorized live assessment bind
the published scope to its immutable release source. Product metadata is
1.0.0 on this commit. The operator records the publication date and matching
tag/GitHub release after the required deployed checks. A version label does
not replace missing financial or device evidence.
