## What this release makes possible

**Your money carries its proof. Its source, ownership and history stay connected as it moves.**

Reality-Grade Banking is the open receiving standard for the Proof-Native Economy. Receiz provides Reality-Grade Infrastructure. Bankeiz puts that standard in people's hands: one account to add money, send it, receive it, invest, meet and act with the context already connected.

The everyday result is simple: understand what you can afford, see what is owed and due, get paid, pay people and keep the record. Open the details and follow the exact units, calculation, authorization and completed result. Independent implementations follow the published conformance laws to preserve that continuity.

Version 4 connects the account, Originals, shares, Vaults, agent, contacts, Calendar, booking and meetings. The interface stays simple while the evidence stays inspectable. Each Original carries its own signing, public-proof and ownership requirements. Signing one does not unlock another. Current ownership and accepted source history govern use. A retained account signature milestone records historical acceptance; it is not a current spending permission.

The existing relationship remains inspectable:

~~~text
Remaining Original Phi at t =

~~~

Completed payments retain their executed amount, units, quote and coordinate. Later source valuation does not rewrite those payments. Transferring the Original and sending a specified amount of money remain distinct supported operations.
