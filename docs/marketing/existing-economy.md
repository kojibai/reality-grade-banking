# Existing value, a useful next exchange

The owner’s October 26 brief and approved review govern this suite. Seven new routes join the preserved cooperative suite: `/economy/`, `/farmers/`, `/fuel/`, `/repairs/`, `/grocers/`, `/housing/`, `/workers/`, plus the existing `/cooperatives/` alias of `/coops/`.

## The five switches

Recognize existing work and assets. Identify who needs them. Show how a commonly accepted monetary unit connects separate exchanges without requiring a perfect reciprocal swap. Show the recipient's next use. Establish independent organizational implementation and governance. The cooperative director asks how much members can already provide to one another.

The main story is human first. Dollar amounts supply a familiar reference for the practical purchase; exact Phi units, Settlement, source, authority, conversion and receiving remain in the financial detail. A source valuation never becomes cash funding or accepted liquidity through a label. The examples are public presentation only, with no SDK writes, model generation or account reads.

## Numbers and public sources

The interactive example uses EIA’s [weekly U.S. on-highway No. 2 diesel series](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist/LeafHandler.ashx?n=PET&s=EMD_EPD2D_PTE_NUS_DPG&f=W): October 19, 2026 observation $6.276 per gallon, published October 20, checked October 26. This is a national retail figure including taxes, not an Iowa merchant offer or off-road fuel price. The reference is a dated build-time snapshot; no runtime price-fetch latency is added.

At the default 100 gallons, the illustrative fuel purchase is $627.60. Separate repair, grocery and produce orders assume the same agreed reference price to make circulation understandable. Four successive purchases illustrate $2,510.40 of gross payments with the initial $627.60 of available reference value conserved. This is neither value creation nor four times the money, income, profit, savings or a loan multiplier. The model assumes one fixed conversion basis, sufficient actual spendable value, voluntary acceptance, no fees and actual deliveries. Outside obligations retain their terms. The calculator is not a quote or financial ledger.

Public service references, checked October 26: [Berry Patch Farm](https://berrypatchfarm.com/) describes produce and a Nevada, Iowa farm store; [Key Cooperative](https://www.keycoop.com/energy) describes fuel/energy services; [Campus Garage](https://campusgarage.com/) describes Ames automotive repair; [Wheatsfield Co-op](https://www.wheatsfield.coop/history/) describes its member-owned grocery and local-economy mission. Their services illustrate the roles. No source establishes their participation, acceptance policy, mutual trading relationship, endorsement or any example invoice. Names appear in optional public-reference detail with that scope explicit; no logos or testimonial claims are used.
