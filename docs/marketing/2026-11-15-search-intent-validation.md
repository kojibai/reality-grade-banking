# Search intent validation for the independent resource launch

Research date: **November 15, 2026, Eastern Time**, through approximately 9:15 p.m. Measurement market to establish: **United States, English**. This is search-result and first-party resource research, not a measured keyword opportunity report.

The independent launch can publish useful templates and problem guides before the separate client-work feature merge. Lead with copyable material and original fictional examples. Reference only verified, already released Messages, Business, booking and payment actions. Do not advertise a Bankeiz proposal/invoice generator, agreement-signing workflow, free AI, automatic sending or an unreleased feature.

Read alongside the [growth plan](2026-11-15-organic-growth-plan.md) and [launch backlog](2026-11-15-organic-launch-backlog.md). This review samples **24 resource/problem clusters**, not all 40 planned research clusters or all 512 candidates. Independent owners use /learn/templates/ and /learn/resources/; check existing ownership before adding a URL.

## Evidence and limits

All exact strings below were submitted to web search in batches of four on November 15 ET. **S** links appeared in that original query batch; **R** links came from supplemental first-party lookups. “Match” describes task relevance in the returned sample, not monthly demand. Batch results are not a recorded Google top ten. The tool did not supply a controlled US session, complete ads/AI-feature inventory or reproducible positions.

Selected Bonsai, HoneyBook, PandaDoc, ClickUp, Hootsuite, Stripe, Adobe, Teamwork, PostRoom, Orca and Wesley pages were opened to check format. Other observations use indexed excerpts. Smartsheet rejected a fetch, Upwork returned 403, Ocoya returned 404 and MentPass could not be fetched; excerpts do not prove current availability. Fetch status does not establish Google index status.

**Every row has these unmet measurement requirements:** monthly searches/range, country trend, CPC, organic difficulty, Bankeiz impressions/clicks/CTR/position, selected canonical, search-to-activation rate and retained use are **unmeasured**. No connected Search Console or licensed keyword dataset was available. A query found in indexed content is topical evidence, not measured demand. A sparse result sample proves neither unmet demand nor low competition.

Priority means relative **editorial fit**, never a ranking score: **A**, first useful cohort with a concrete recurring task; **B**, selective publication because competition, audience ambiguity or review needs weaken initial priority; **C**, validate wording/ownership before treating it as a standalone acquisition target. Proposed artifacts must be original.

## Research gate

**Approve useful, unmeasured editorial utility:** A resources can enter the first small cohort after artifact, factual and product review. B resources may publish when their original example and audience/reviewer needs are resolved. C guidance remains useful within the library while wording/ownership is researched. This does not approve high-volume, easy-ranking or paid-conversion claims.

First emphasis: proposal follow-up, scope changes, deliverable feedback/social approval, project handover, website SOW and copywriting brief. Each supplies a concrete answer and inspectable artifact without the feature merge. These are usefulness/fit hypotheses. Usable competing artifacts mean “template gap” must describe a demonstrated original contribution, not an empty market.

**Require commercial validation before scaling:** high-traffic/low-difficulty claims, profession expansion, paid-plan conversion, generator head terms and the two mixed-intent C targets. This review supplies no demand total supporting the million-visit scenario. Page count cannot substitute for addressable demand.

## Day-one measurement capture

1. In the authorized Search Console property, choose Performance → Search results → Web. Export the latest complete 90 days and prior comparable period; record reporting lag/final date. Export query/page totals, then filter each landing page and export its queries. Segment US, country and device deliberately; do not invent query/page pairs by joining unrelated totals. Separate branded queries. Capture clicks, impressions, CTR and aggregate average position. [Google Performance guidance](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en).
2. In Keyword Planner, submit these strings and observed variants. Set United States, English and Google; record settings/window. Export actual estimates/ranges, monthly trends, bids/CPC and variant groupings. Preserve untouched dated exports and a deduplicated cluster sheet. Advertising competition measures advertisers, not organic difficulty; forecasts are not observed traffic. Access may require account/billing setup. [Google metric guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575?hl=en), [historical metric definitions](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/keyword-planning/generate-historical-metrics).
3. Recheck leading candidates in a controlled US/English session on mobile/desktop. Save actual URLs, formats, date, ads/AI features and overlap; inspect five relevant pages per selected cluster. Add a licensed organic dataset only when available, preserving market/date. Resolve duplicate intent before another URL.
4. Maintain one row per canonical owner with blank unknowns. Connect organic landing cohorts to permitted artifact interaction, a relevant released action and seven-day return, excluding private message/document content. Delivery, indexing, acquisition and activation remain separate evidence.

**Falsifiable update rule:** after the first complete 28-day window, prioritize an existing relevant query/page over new unmeasured content when it has at least 100 US nonbrand impressions, average position 5–20 and a demonstrated answer/artifact defect. These are operating thresholds, not statistical guarantees. If none qualifies, retain a small exploratory cohort. At days 60–90, expand a cluster only when observed queries match the intended task and at least one relevant released action has completed; otherwise diagnose intent, delivery and conversion before adding siblings. Zero early clicks alone does not justify deletion.
