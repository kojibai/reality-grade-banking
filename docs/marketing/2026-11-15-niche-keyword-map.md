# Bankeiz niche → problem → exact search keyword → solution map

Date: 2026-11-15. This is a **finite initial map of 128 credible audience hypotheses across 16 families**, not every possible profession or evidence that every audience should be targeted. Each niche has one concrete problem, four exact candidate search strings, a realistic deliverable, a conversation workflow and a page allocation. The accompanying [JSON array](2026-11-15-niche-keyword-map.json) contains all 128 records with stable IDs.

**Every keyword is `seed_unmeasured`.** The 512 strings below are curated query candidates, not proven search terms or evidence of customer demand. No search-volume, keyword-difficulty, ranking, conversion or competitor claim is made. No external keyword research was performed for this map. Validate demand and product fit before indexing, creating a page or treating a keyword as a campaign target.

## Positioning and common product path

**Run your client relationships from one conversation.** Messages establish the relationship; a private Twin workspace helps the person prepare the next step from the context and history they choose. Twin is an AI counterpart, not an assumption that the entire relationship history is always placed in a prompt. The person reviews the result and decides what to share or do.

The full path is: client messages → selected context in private Twin → reviewed branded proposal → full reviewed agreement → **explicit Receiz ID signature by each person who signs** → appointments and invoices as relevant → **separately approved payment actions** → delivery → the next conversation in the continued relationship. A signature does not authorize a payment. Drafts, sends and payments are not autonomous. Real deliverable files can be PDF, DOCX, XLSX and PPTX; each row names the formats that help that audience. Not every engagement needs every step or file type.

Public proof must preserve the actual existing supported source and continuity. A presentation, screenshot, display JSON, extracted hash or fresh delivery response does not replace an enclosing Original or create proof authority. Where a row mentions artwork, source or proof, it means the actual retained supported records, with their known limitations, not a new authenticity, ownership, escrow or copyright guarantee.

These workflows do not imply broad external integrations, CRM synchronization, automatic platform publishing, tax or legal compliance, clinical functionality, escrow, guaranteed collections, guaranteed results or a new regulated capability. A regulated-adjacent operator using administrative documents still has to qualify its actual obligations; the regulated/public-sector family remains Discovery only.

## Demand and page-creation gate

1. Interview the actual provider and buyer about a recent paid job, the documents they used, the language they searched and the point where the relationship broke down. Keep the problem hypothesis if the interview contradicts the proposed query; revise the query rather than manufacture demand.
2. Research the exact candidate strings and observed variants with dated, attributable keyword/SERP evidence. Record country, language and intent. Separate observed query data from tool estimates and distinguish workflow intent from free-template intent. Do not promote `seed_unmeasured` to a demand claim without evidence.
3. Demonstrate the requested workflow with real reviewed files, explicit individual signatures and separate payment approval as applicable. Validate the actual supported behavior and any professional restrictions; a plausible use case is not proof of capability.
4. Allocate confirmed demand to the existing matching `/business/for/*` page. Merge queries that answer the same task on the same page. Create a new path only when distinct intent, useful original examples and a demonstrable audience workflow justify it; avoid near-duplicate niche pages.
5. Prioritize using measured demand, interview evidence, buying intent and successful workflow evidence. None of those measurements exist in this seed map. Discovery audiences require a separate suitability decision before public marketing.
