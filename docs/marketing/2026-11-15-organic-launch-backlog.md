# Bankeiz first month organic publication backlog

November 16–December 15, 2026, conditional on the qualified client-work release. This is a maximum of 60 distinct page changes, including improvements to existing pages. Keyword phrases are unmeasured research candidates. Validate demand, intent overlap and product fit before publication. Proposed paths are design candidates, not routes that already exist. Merge overlapping intents instead of filling a quota.

Each brief must record its evidence source/date/market, intent owner, concrete example, product reviewer, real next action and expected activation event. Product pages and niche pages require a content operator plus product review; agreement/legal guidance needs the appropriate qualified review. Use a consented customer case only when permission and real evidence exist; otherwise label the example fictional.

## Publication and learning

Do not publish this backlog wholesale. Promote each brief after evidence and product review, then publish in small cohorts. At release record the canonical, source revision, compiled validation, CI and live HTTP result. Search Console index selection, queries and clicks belong in the later acquisition report. They cannot be inferred from a successful build or sitemap submission.

Update existing near-ranking pages as soon as data identifies them, even if that displaces a new page in this queue. Review overlap between a template, a guide and a product page before producing each. Comparisons and localized pages join later when tested evidence and the measured opportunity justify them.
