# Holding value and retaining continuity

Holding is protected custody of verified sources and admitted rights. A price display, cache entry, downloaded image or successful login is not sufficient by itself.

## Custody contract

- Preserve complete original proof objects and unknown namespaces byte-exactly. Store private material encrypted under the intended identity and operating environment. Keep public verification separate from private custody access.
- Retain proof-carried ownership, applicable backing/allocation and the exact current causal head. Reverify on import, restoration and each process/trust-boundary crossing. JSON serialization does not transfer SDK WeakMap or native device custody.
- Keep signing keys and qualified hardware capabilities in their native protected environment. An Identity Seal backup does not restore a note's device-bound qualified custody. Document supported hardware, recovery limits and who can operate the receiving device.
- Hold the original canonical intent, semantic idempotency coordinate, application, participant heads, rail and amount before irreversible work. Protect the exact pending state with compare-and-swap and source retention so another tab/reload cannot invent a second intent.
- Resume the original operation after interruption. Resolve unknown funding, consumption and credit outcomes with authenticated exact-intent recovery. Do not abandon a possibly committed operation because a response timed out, an account projection changed or a GET returned no row.
- Retain verified terminal evidence and the original sources after redemption. A consumed note remains historical evidence and cannot be counted again as available value.

## Time and valuation

Display local time for people. Carry and validate Kai/KKS coordinates with their verified KaiSigil proof for native history and causal admission. Preserve exact micro-unit values and canonical deterministic USD projections. A display timestamp, cached price or generated chart point cannot authorize a transfer.

## Availability and first paint

Paint the last independently verified local projection immediately when available. Indicate observation freshness accurately; background refresh may append a new observation without rebuilding history. A retained source can remain inspectable during a service outage. Live spendability and a qualified route must still be checked before an operation that requires them.

Cache public verifier resources and application assets separately from private account API calls. Never cache bearer material, replay payment requests in a service worker or treat offline availability of the UI as evidence of supported offline custody.

## Operational recovery exercises

Before live qualification, interrupt at each allocation, handoff, consumption and credit boundary. Restart the process, reopen the original pending state and prove the exact same intent resolves without duplicate debit/credit. Exercise stale heads, concurrent duplicate deposits, user cancellation, revoked consent, unavailable synchronization and corrupted/missing sources. Evidence must include the independently verified post-recovery sources, not only server logs.
